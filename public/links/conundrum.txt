A space that explores lesser-known-soundscape performances and creates sonic journeys by visiting underground, left-field, and cerebral artistry with an aim to teleport the listener to paradoxical lands. The formation of a vital space to highlight unconventional performances, the latest and future methodologies in live music. Sonic rituals curated by Omnivoid Labs designed to disorient and transcend with audio visual convergences.
